The story so far.

Rec. Punjab, India
Playing since MMXIV
Home base Vancouver, BC
01

Born in Punjab.

Music was the room's second language — dhol on the radio, film songs from the kitchen, a father's cassette collection stacked by the door.

Origin · ਪੰਜਾਬ
02

Mixtapes for the road.

Collecting cassette tapes and crafting playlists for family trips — the first sets ever mixed were for a car full of relatives, none of whom would sit still for a bad transition.

First rig · Sony Walkman
03

Landed in Canada.

The roots stayed — competitive Bhangra teams on weekends, custom mixes for teammates on weekdays, one foot in the diaspora and one back home. That double-life is still the sound.

New home · ਵੈਨਕੂਵਰ
04

A decade behind the decks.

Ten-plus years of professional DJing has made Apogee a fixture of the South Asian wedding and event scene across the Lower Mainland — live mixing, crowd reading, custom playlists built with each client in the room.

Est. MMXIV
END OF SIDE A
With his grounded roots and heartfelt passion to the forefront — this is Apogee.
— Liner note, Flying Nimbus
